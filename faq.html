Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers regarding BlogStore orders, delivery, AMP performance, and Schema.org specs.

❓ What multi-niche departments does BlogStore support?

BlogStore supports Grocery, Hardware, Fast-food, Vegetables, Salon & Spa, and Electronics with dedicated JSON-LD Schema.org types (ProductGroup, Product, Offer, BeautySalon, MenuItem).

🌐 How do currency and RTL locale direction toggles work?

The top navigation bar contains real-time AMP state bindings that dynamically format prices across USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), and AED (د.إ), and update page direction between LTR and RTL.

⚡ Is this store 100% valid AMP HTML?

Yes! All stylesheets are compiled directly into a single custom AMP style block using UnoCSS presetWind3 and checked with amphtml-validator.
